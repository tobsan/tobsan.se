## Goodbye Twitter

When twitter was first bought by Elon Musk, I first laughed at the
ridiculousness surrounding the purchase. Then, things quickly got out of hand,
so I deleted my twitter account about a year ago.

I did miss a reasonable text-based social media though, so I tried out Threads.
And while it's not so bad technically, the posts that one gets to see made it
feel like a feed-version of [familjeliv.se](familjeliv.se), and that's not a
good thing. Plus the TikTok-y system for choosing posts made it even worse.

## Hello, mastodon!
I finally did what a computer science graduate should have done a **long** time
ago, I got me a mastodon account and joined the fediverse. I feel a bit like
when I had joined twitter and started tweeting with my fellow students' union
colleagues back in 2010, it's **amazing**. Plus, I get to own all my own data, I
can migrate to another instance, or self-host an instance etc etc.

You may follow me on [https://fosstodon.org/@tobsan](https://fosstodon.org/@tobsan).
Notice how I've set up this site to link there in the footer as well, pretty
neat, right?
