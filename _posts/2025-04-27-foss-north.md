year's conference.

Next year's foss-north will be on April 27-28th.

## Importance of FOSS
Probably preaching to the choir here, but it's worth repeating what
[Daniel](https://daniel.haxx.se) said in his
[talk](https://foss-north.se/2025/speakers-and-talks.html#dstenberg) about cURL:

> 96% of commercial code contains Open Source

> Firms would need to spend 3.5 times
> more on software than they currently do if OSS did not exist

Yet, most spend little or no money on maintaining och contributing to open
source software. I believe we can do better!

So, what can you do? A non-exhaustive list:
1. Contribute to FOSS, by upstreaming patches, improving software you're already
   using, and so on.
1. Contribute financially to projects you're using. Maintenance is often done in
   somebody's spare time; helping out financially could enable the maintainers
   to do more.

Sponsoring and joining events such as foss-north is **also** a good thing, since
it enables a place for FOSS users and developers to meet up, and some of the
money we get from sponsors and tickets goes to supporting speakers with travel
costs etc. We have had to cut speakers from the conf for financial reasons
before, and it's never feels good.
