A bit more than a month ago I scheduled my facebook account for deletion. It's
now gone, along with messenger. I hadn't used facebook for anything more than
doomscrolling and the occational news about what's happening where I live for a
long time.

There are many good reasons to leave facebook, some more obvious than other:
- While facebook has some features to protect against abuse, these are hardly
  ever used for that purpose, and instead they are used to silence those with
  divergent opinions.
- Facebook's owners have shown many times that they don't care about the public
  good, power is much more important to them than to provide a useful platform.
- The general enshittification of Facebook.
- If you have a facebook account, you can be tracked in many places on the
  web.

What really tipped me over the edge though, was that when I opted out of having
personalised ads, I instead was forced to have an ad-break every now and then
(this is the case on Instagram as well btw, where I still have an account for
now), giving a feeling very much like the [black mirror
episode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fifteen_Million_Merits).

## Effects of leaving
The general family chat with my brothers, parents and in-laws runs through
messenger. However, my parents tend to call when they want something, and I can
obviously text my brothers.

What I already feel like I'm missing out on, are contacts with people who aren't
exactly essential, but where my main source of contact with them have been
facebook. I will miss these small updates.

## Addressing the hypocrisy
Now, I'm aware that keeping a whatsapp and instagram account doesn't exactly
make sense if I want to break free from the metaverse (lol). I'm likely to keep
whatsapp due to its widespread utility use when I go abroad. When I was in Peru,
whatsapp seemed to be the main communications tool.

As for instagram, it's likely just a question of time. I guess I could post my
food and DDR images on Mastodon instead. It's a huge time sink.

I'm open for suggestions for good things to use instead of the tech giants'
offerings.
